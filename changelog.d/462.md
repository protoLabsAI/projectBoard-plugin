- **The loop tick no longer waits on one card's review, merge or gate work (#462).** The PR
  reconcile ran every `in_review` card's rebase, merged-state gate, CI check, review gate and
  auto-merge inline, ahead of the claim scan. A 600 s gate and a review call whose model
  stream had hung kept a board with 32 ready cards and 0 in progress from claiming anything
  for about four hours. Each card's reconcile now runs as its own tracked task, like a drive,
  at most `reconcile_concurrency` (default 2) at once across repos. One repo's cards stay
  serial, so sibling PRs can't both merge on a stale merged-state stamp. A card that queues
  settles a merged or closed PR first and is re-read when its turn comes. A card still in a
  reconcile is skipped on the next poll, and shutdown cancels them with their gate process
  trees. The tick stays O(seconds).
- **The review-gate model call has a hard timeout of the board's own,
  `review_gate_timeout_s` (default 1800).** The host client's `request_timeout` did not
  apply to a hung stream (protoAgent#3699). On timeout the board abandons the call and
  returns at once, even if the call ignores its cancel. The card stays `review-pending`
  without spending `review_run_max`, and no second call starts for it while the abandoned
  one still runs.
- **A board that stops claiming says so.** When there are ready cards and a free drive slot
  but no claim scan has finished for `claim_stall_ticks` ticks (default 10), `/status`
  carries a `claim_stall` line naming the tick phase that is stuck. The same line appears
  as `setup.claim_stall_hint` and in the host's setup-gap warnings.
