- **A green merged-state re-verify no longer spends the `merged_verify_max` budget (#490).**
  On a busy repo each sibling merge moved base, and each move cost the waiting card one
  re-verify even when its merged state passed. Five green PRs parked as `budget exhausted`
  overnight. Now only a re-verify that reaches no verdict (the gate timed out, was killed or
  could not launch) or a red one counts, and a real green verdict resets the count. The
  exhaustion sentinel works as before for what still counts. The board listing, `/features`
  and `board_get` also read the cap from the running loop, the one that wrote the sentinel,
  and fall back to the configured value when no loop runs. A card the loop had parked could
  read `auto-merge pending` before.
