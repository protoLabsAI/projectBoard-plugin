A provider that can't serve its model is now rotated past instead of climbed on. A retired
model, one the account's plan refuses, or one that needs a newer client used to classify as
`terminal`, so the loop escalated a tier on a provider failure — and a dead provider listed at
every rung was re-picked at each one while a working sibling sat idle. These refusals are now
their own `provider_unavailable` class: the loop switches to the next provider at the same
rung, remembers the dead one for 30 minutes so later cards skip it, and when no provider on the
rung can serve its model it blocks under `dispatch-infra` naming the delegates, without climbing.
