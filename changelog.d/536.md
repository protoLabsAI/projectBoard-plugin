- **Done/cancelled cards stop alerting, and close edges clear their stale block/review
  labels (#536).** Operators were paged for cards that had already shipped — bd-g1d3 after
  PR #4184 merged, bd-b93u after #4177, nerdsville bd-5go after #41 — because
  `_notify_operator` deduped on an incident key but never asked whether the card was still
  open. It now re-reads the card right before writing the inbox row and DROPS the alert (at
  debug) for a `done`/`cancelled` card; a re-read that raises FAILS OPEN so a real block is
  never hidden. The re-read goes through the same store the sweep is already driving, and the
  blocked sweep now drives the whole notify through `asyncio.to_thread` — the re-read is a
  blocking `br show`, so an inline call stalled every coroutine and logged the on-loop warning
  for each escalated card, every pass (#258). Closed cards also kept stale labels (protoAgent
  bd-b4pr was `done` but still read `blocked: true`): `record_merge`, `mark_done`, and
  `cancel_feature` removed only `blocked` and left `blocked-class:*`, `in-review`,
  `review-pending` and `changes-requested` behind. All three now shed every live-state label
  through one shared `_terminal_stale_labels` helper, keeping the `cancelled` tag, the
  `review-clean` verdict and audit labels (`verified:`, `merged-verified:`, `self-verified`,
  …). All three strip BEFORE `br close`, so all three are now atomic-or-clean: a close that
  fails or times out-while-open re-adds exactly the stripped labels, keeping a still-open card
  in the `in_review`/`blocked` lane the merge/blocked poll retries on (a card dropped to plain
  `in_progress` fell out of both retry sets); a close that timed out but actually landed is
  detected by re-read and keeps its labels shed, so a `done` card never reads as a live block
  again. `board_list(state="blocked")` is deliberately untouched — board_state maps the
  `blocked` FLAG, so a live blocked card is still listed and a `blocked-class:terminal` label
  with no flag stays a stale class, not a live block (#401).
