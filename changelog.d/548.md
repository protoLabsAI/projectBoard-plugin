- **The Ready gate now warns when a card's SPEC reads wide, not just its file list (#548).** The breadth cap counts `files_to_modify`, but a conceptually wide card can name few files and still time out before it lands — ds-syt (protoContent#609) had 4 files at difficulty medium and timed out twice at 1800s. A new non-blocking `spec-width` advisory rides beside the dependency advisory in `ready_check(...)["advisories"]` (so it surfaces automatically at `board_create_feature` / `board_update_feature`) when the spec is over `SPEC_WIDTH_MAX_CHARS` (2500) chars, the acceptance criteria exceed `SPEC_WIDTH_MAX_CRITERIA` (6), or the spec has more than `SPEC_WIDTH_MAX_JOINS` (3) "and also"-style concern joins. It names every signal that tripped with its measured value and threshold, suggests splitting into one-concern cards, and never refuses — `mark_ready` still promotes the card. Task-type beads are skipped, and it runs even for a card with no files. Thresholds are tunable module constants beside `max_files_by_difficulty`.
