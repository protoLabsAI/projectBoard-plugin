- **Coders are told to search inside their worktree, not the whole disk.** A coder that
  couldn't find a file locally would fall back to a filesystem-wide `find`/`bfs` from `/`.
  That never finds what it wants (a dependency's source is missing because a fresh
  worktree has no `node_modules`, not because it sits elsewhere), it costs minutes of the
  card's clock, and it walks every app's data on the box — which makes macOS ask the
  operator to approve "access data from other apps" in the agent's name.
