Coder dispatch now steers by subscription headroom when a rung lists several interchangeable
delegates. A card opens on the sibling with the most headroom instead of one already at or above
`routing.prefer_sibling_at`% of its binding window (default 90), or one the host reports unusable
until its reset, and a provider failure rotates to the highest-headroom sibling left. Usage is read
only through the host's `graph.sdk.provider_usage`, which is getattr-guarded: an older host without
it, a sibling with no snapshot, or `routing.headroom: false` all fall back byte-for-byte to the prior
round-robin behaviour. Steering reorders only within the exclusive-filtered rung, so it never selects
a delegate outside `routing.exclusive`. Both knobs apply at board and project scope and reload live.
