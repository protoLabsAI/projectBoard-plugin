- **A gate preflight whose command is missing no longer parks cards as `terminal`
  (protoLabsAI/protoAgent#3585).** When a project's `local_gate_cmd` pointed at an
  interpreter that no longer exists (an absolute `<checkout>/.venv/bin/python` whose venv
  was removed), the preflight hold stamped every ready card `blocked-class:terminal`,
  "needs a human, never clears", although the card was fine and the loop lifts the hold by
  itself once the gate runs again. Preflight holds now carry their own
  `blocked-class:preflight-hold`; the work snapshot, the dispatch record and the operator
  alert say to fix the project's gate environment and that the hold then releases itself,
  and the sweep still never requeues such a card. A gate that exits "command not found"
  (127, or cmd.exe's 9009) or cannot launch now ends its reason with a line naming the
  missing command and the project whose `local_gate_cmd` to fix.
