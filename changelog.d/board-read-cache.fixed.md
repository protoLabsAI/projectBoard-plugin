- **Board reads are fast with several viewers open, even while the board is working a card (#688).** The board page, the in-chat board cards and every other card listing now read from one cached copy of the board. Before, each request read the whole board history again and waited in line behind the work loop, so `/cards` could take up to 19 seconds and six open boards waited more than 10 seconds each. The copy refreshes after any change. A change you make shows up on your next read. If the board is busy, you get the last copy within about a second and the page reads again moments later.
