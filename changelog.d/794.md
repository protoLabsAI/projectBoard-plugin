- **A slow health sweep no longer stops ready cards from being claimed (#794).** The sweep used to run inside the loop's tick, ahead of the claim scan, so several slow GitHub calls in a row held every claim: on 2026-11-18 the board claimed nothing for 303 s and then 528 s with ready cards and free slots, and the log did not say which step was slow. Now the tick waits at most 5 s for the sweep and then goes on to claim while the sweep finishes on its own. A sweep step that takes more than 5 s is logged by name with its seconds (`sweep step worktree reap <repo> took 121s`), and the claim-stall warning on `/status` names the step the sweep is in. The sweep's cadence, its failure handling and its shutdown are unchanged; a store stall in a sweep that had left its tick ends the next tick.
