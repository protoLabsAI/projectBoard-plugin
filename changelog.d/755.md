- **Retired work locations and a done card's closed work stop raising recovery alerts (#755).**
  The location sweep probed every recorded work location on every card, including done and
  cancelled ones. After a PR merged and its worktree was removed on purpose, the probe reported
  the path `missing` (or hit an `ls-remote` error) and the card showed `[recovery] Recorded work
  needs recovery` forever. Work-location entries now take an optional boolean `retired` field: a
  retired entry is never probed and raises no risk. The sweep also skips every entry on a
  terminal (done/cancelled) card — unless it records real unpushed work (`pushed: false` with a
  `sha`), whose sole local copy still alerts. Skipped entries and their cached probes are dropped
  from the recovery view, so a closed card with only such entries shows no recovery row after the
  next sweep. An `ls-remote` failure is now an explicit probe error (status `unknown`), never
  reported as unpushed work.
