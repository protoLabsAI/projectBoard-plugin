A `br` call that stalls no longer takes the loop down, and no handler treats a stall as a
definite answer. A stall is now a board error that names the call. The stalled `br` is asked to
stop (SIGTERM) and then killed along with anything it forked, and the timeout (now 45s) sits
above `br`'s own 30-second lock wait. Each phase of a tick runs on its own, so a failed phase
costs only itself. A stalled store ends the tick after one call and the next tick tries again;
a wedged store used to make seven stalled calls per tick. A stall is also no longer mistaken
for a lost claim race (which could terminal-block a healthy card), for a failed cancel (whose
undo could turn a cancelled card into a "done" one), or for an empty board (so a create no
longer goes ahead blind). A block is never recorded without its reason, the API answers a
stall with 503, and the loop's state-filtered reads fetch card detail only for the rows they
return, at most 100 per call. They used to `br show` all ~350 cards on the board in one call.
