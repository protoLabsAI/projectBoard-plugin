A `br` call that stalls no longer takes the whole loop tick down with it. A timeout is now a
board error that names the call. Each phase of the tick (PR reconcile, health sweep, preflight,
claim scan) runs on its own, so a stalled read in one no longer skips the others, and a failed
preflight still holds the claim scan. The loop's state-filtered reads now fetch card detail only
for the rows they return, in batches of at most 100. They used to `br show` every card on the
board, and on a live board that was about 350 ids in one call. Four ticks in ten minutes had
done nothing because of it.
