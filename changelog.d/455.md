- **Generated files and changesets no longer count toward the breadth cap.** A one-token
  change in protoContent touches `tokens.js`, the committed `dist/tokens.css` and
  `dist/tokens.json`, and a mandatory `.changeset/*.md`: four files, already at the medium
  cap, so every test or story forced a split. The new per-project `breadth_exclude` globs
  (top level = every project's fallback) leave files nobody authors out of the count. The
  defaults are `.changeset/**`, lockfiles (`pnpm-lock.yaml`, `package-lock.json`,
  `yarn.lock`, `uv.lock`, `poetry.lock`, `Cargo.lock`), `**/dist/**`, `*.generated.*`,
  `CHANGELOG.md` and `changelog.d/**`. A configured list replaces them, and the word
  `defaults` inside it keeps them. Excluded files still reach the coder brief.
- **The Ready gate reports every failure at once, and runs when a card is written.**
  Both live boards went through create, refused, fix, refused again for a different
  reason, then cancel and split. Now:
  - `board_mark_ready` names every failed check in one refusal, each with its fix.
  - `board_create_feature`, `board_create_task` and `board_update_feature` dry-run every
    check that can be judged statically (required fields, paths exist or are `(new)`,
    breadth, design, shared files, dependency sanity) and return it as `ready_check`,
    with `will_be_refused_at_ready` listing each failure and its fix. The card is still
    saved.
  - New tool `board_check_ready` and route `GET /features/{fid}/ready-check` report the
    same without changing anything.
  - An edge onto a cancelled card is flagged as an advisory: it released without its work
    landing.
