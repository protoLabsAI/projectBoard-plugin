Fresh-build coder timeouts that follow a long silence — no tool, thought, plan or answer for at least `provider_stall_idle_s` (default 600) seconds before the kill — now record `provider_stall` and retry on the same tier without spending the too-wide timeout budget. The idle gap is measured from the live coder stream (the progress monitor), so it fires on the board's own dispatches, not just hosts that bake an age into the timeout message. Retries use the durable `budget:provider-stall` counter, capped by `provider_stall_max` (default 2), then resume existing timeout handling. Known idle gaps appear in timeout reasons (`last event Ns before timeout`); unknown gaps and pre-model failures retain existing behavior.
