Fresh-build coder timeouts with a reported last-tool-event age of at least `provider_stall_idle_s` (default 600 seconds) now record `provider_stall` and retry on the same tier without spending the too-wide timeout budget. Retries use the durable `budget:provider-stall` counter, capped by `provider_stall_max` (default 2), then resume existing timeout handling. Known idle ages appear in timeout reasons; missing or malformed ages and pre-model failures retain existing behavior.
