Classify a spent provider allowance as `rate_limit` so the drive rotates to the rung's live sibling instead of blocking the card. Codex's exhausted weekly quota reports `You've hit your usage limit … try again at <date>` with `codexErrorInfo: usageLimitExceeded` — no "rate limit", no "quota" and no 429, so it matched no rule and fell through to `terminal`. Since `terminal` is not rotatable, a card blocked with its Claude sibling idle beside it, which is the whole arrangement the rung ladder exists for. Also covers the `out of usage credits` phrasing.
