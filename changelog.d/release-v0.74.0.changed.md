- **Three chat commands for the operator: `/standup`, `/triage-github` and `/grill-me`.** `/standup` writes a status report from the board and open PRs: epics as phases, the critical path, and what needs your call. `/triage-github` ranks an issue queue by Value, Level of Effort and Risk, skips issues already on the board, and can turn chosen quick wins into cards when asked. `/grill-me` stress-tests a plan one question at a time and creates nothing until you confirm. All three use the board's and GitHub's tools, with no shell steps (#665).
