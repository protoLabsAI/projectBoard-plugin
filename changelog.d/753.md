A coder dispatch that times out now leaves the lead something to work from (#753): before
the drive discards the tree or blocks/parks the card, it records the timed-out worktree in
the card's work-location ledger (`timeout-worktree`, with its branch and HEAD sha), so
`board_list_work_locations` shows where the partial work lives. The block or too-wide park
reason keeps its `coder timed out after …` first line and adds the worktree path and a tail
(at most 1000 characters) of the timed-out gen's last tool, reasoning and output. A store
that refuses the record is logged and never changes what happens to the card.
