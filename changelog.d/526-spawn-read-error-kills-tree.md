- **A read error on a child's output no longer leaks its process tree.** The guarded spawn (#526) killed and reaped the whole process group only on a timeout or a cancel. An OSError from a pipe read, a decode error, or any other exception raised while the board read a child's output skipped the kill and left the tree running. The project-registration gate smoke fell through to its outer `except OSError`, and `git`/`gh` caught only the two. Every spawn-and-read path now shares one cleanup: any exit other than a normal return kills the group and reaps it. That covers the loop's gates, preflight, fixups and acceptance tests, the registration gate smoke, `git`, `gh`, and the `lsof` cwd probe.
