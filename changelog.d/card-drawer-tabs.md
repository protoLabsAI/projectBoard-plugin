- **Every card opens, and a building card shows its code.** Clicking any coding card (or pressing Enter on it) opens the drawer, not just cards being built. The drawer has two tabs. **Card** shows the card itself: state, priority, difficulty, project, base, runs, PR, dependencies, publish gates, labels, spec, acceptance criteria, the requirement ledger and the planned files. **Code** shows the coder monitor above a new **Changes** section: every file the card's worktree differs in from its base (committed, uncommitted and untracked), each expanding to its diff. It refreshes every 10 seconds while the tab is open. The Code tab appears for a card being built, and for any other card that still has a run or a worktree. A building card opens on Code, anything else on Card, and the drawer remembers each card's last tab. Secret-looking files are listed without their content. New read-only route: `GET /features/{fid}/changes`.
