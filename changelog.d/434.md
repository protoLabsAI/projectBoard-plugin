The board no longer destroys a worktree that holds work existing nowhere else. A coder that
died before its candidate was promoted left the only copy of its implementation in that tree,
and the next re-dispatch force-removed it and deleted its branch. The merge, cancel, done and
sweep reaps and a shutdown did the same. Such a card now blocks as `stranded-work`, naming
each tree, its branch, what it holds and how to recover or discard it, and no coder runs until
someone decides. Work means any uncommitted change, plus commits on a candidate branch that no
other ref holds. The coder's session scratch and the board's `node_modules` links don't count.
Clean stale trees still reap normally. A restart mid-build now leaves the interrupted tree in
place instead of reaping it.
