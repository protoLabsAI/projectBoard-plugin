- **Status chips on cards stay short.** A PR held for an unclean merge state showed every check in the chip (`held: PR not clean on GitHub (BLOCKED) — verify workspace config: in progress; lint: in progress; …`). It now reads `checks running`, `ci failing` or `merge blocked`. Any other long status is cut to its first clause. The full status is in the chip's tooltip and in a new **status** line at the top of the card drawer, which also works on touch screens.
