- **`br` stops copying the whole board into its history on every write.** The board now runs `br` with `BR_HISTORY_MIN_INTERVAL_SECS=3600` unless you set it yourself, so `br` keeps at most one `.br_history` snapshot of `issues.jsonl` an hour. Before, it snapshotted the file on every write, and on protoEngineer that grew to 1.3–2.8 GB within hours; the disk churn slowed every board operation. Stopgap for #686.
