Coder dispatch can now keep a named account under a target usage percentage. `routing.usage_policy`
is a list of `{account, keep_under, prefer, tiers}` rules: when a matching-tier card's account is at
or above `keep_under`% of its binding window used, the card opens on the rule's `prefer` delegate
instead of today's choice. It is the lowest-precedence routing steer — exclusive > gates >
down/blocked > headroom > policy — so it only reorders within the already exclusive-filtered, live
rung and never selects a delegate outside `routing.exclusive`, outside the rung, or one that is
spent/down/blocked. Account usage is read only through the host's `graph.sdk.provider_usage`; an
unknown snapshot means the rule does not fire, byte-for-byte identical to the headroom behaviour. A
rule naming an unknown delegate or account, or a `keep_under` outside 1–100, is refused with a reason
and leaves the current policy unchanged. Rules apply at board and project scope and reload live.
