When the in-loop review gate exhausts its fix budget, the block reason now tells apart a card where the **same** finding survived every fix round ("review findings persist after N fix attempt(s) — needs human review", and it names the surviving finding(s)) from one where **each round raised a different** finding ("new findings each round after N fix attempt(s) (rounds conflict) — needs a design call; needs human review"). The second case means patching round by round keeps opening the next round's hole (bd-49yo), so the human is told to make a design call rather than attempt another patch. Both wordings keep "fix attempt(s)" and "needs human review", so the operator-unblock block classifier (`_REVIEW_GATE_BLOCK_MARKERS`) still recognizes the block — now keyed off those two shared phrases instead of "review findings persist after", which only the first wording carries.

Review-budget blocks explicitly remain terminal and do not record host friction, regardless of failure-related words in surviving reviewer claims or the "rounds conflict" wording. They stay blocked for a human decision instead of being automatically requeued.
