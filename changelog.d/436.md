A card moved while its build was still running is no longer blocked when that build finishes.
Requeuing a card for another CI-fix round while the loop's own round was in flight used to park
it terminally, with an open PR, on the message "open_review expects in_progress, got 'ready'".
Blocking a card by hand mid-round had the reason you gave replaced by that same message. The
move now stands. The finished build leaves a note on the card and frees its slot, its push is
already on the PR, and a requeued card's next round picks up from that branch.
