A card moved while its build is still running (held, marked done, cancelled, or requeued) now
keeps that move and its work. Before blocking the card, starting another attempt, opening the
PR or handing the card to review, the build checks whether the card is still its own. If it is
not, the build stands aside: nothing is blocked, retried or sent to review. A PR it already
opened is recorded on the card, so the next round builds on it. Work it hadn't pushed stays in
its worktree, and a held or done card gets no PR at all. Before this, a card requeued mid-round
went terminal with an open PR on "open_review expects in_progress, got 'ready'". A hold placed
mid-round could be retried under three times and then lifted by the sweep. Sister-agent and self
task drives follow the same rule: a failed task blocks its card only while it is still in
progress, and never a card the drive could not re-read. The requeue tools and the `/ci` and
`/review` routes now refuse a card the loop is still working, and say to wait for the round to
end or cancel the card.
