- **The board's own review gate no longer collides with the external QA panel, and the
  strictest verdict wins (protoLabsAI/projectBoard-plugin#512).** The in-loop review gate used
  to publish its pending/success/failure commit statuses under the `QA panel` context — the very
  name the external review panel's App check uses — and the #323 trusted-PASS adoption read that
  same context back as though it were the panel's verdict. On careercoach#17 (card bd-ywmm.4) the
  gate spent its fix budget and posted `failure` at a head the external panel then PASSed; every
  reconcile read the board's OWN earlier FAIL and held the card for hours. The gate now publishes
  under its own `board/review-gate` context and writes nothing under `QA panel`. The #323 adoption
  no longer treats any commit status as the PASS signal: it adopts only the configured external
  panel's marker review naming the PR's live head with a non-blocking verdict, and with no external
  panel configured it adopts nothing. Before adopting, the strictest verdict wins. The PRIMARY veto
  is the card's OWN local review verdict: a `changes-requested` whose `reviewed-head:<sha>` stamp
  names the live head is the board's own gate rejecting that exact head — held, even if the gate's
  `failure` status never reached GitHub; a stamp naming a different head is a stale rejection a push
  moved out from under (adoption may proceed); a `changes-requested` with no stamp fails closed. The
  SECONDARY, belt-and-braces veto reads the board's own `board/review-gate` status tri-state — a
  completed `failure`/`error` holds, an unreadable read fails closed this pass, and a `pending` or
  absent status does not veto (a leftover `pending` is the inert-gate exit's, the very absent-verdict
  case the adoption repairs). A held card records one `auto-merge held:` comment per (card, head)
  naming the internal-vs-external disagreement and the operator unblock/override needed to merge. A
  stale `QA panel` status an older board left can neither block nor promote a card.
