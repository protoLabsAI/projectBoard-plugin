Pause new claims after the tick loop detects machine sleep from the wall-clock gap minus the monotonic gap. The configurable `wake_settle_gap_s` threshold defaults to 60 seconds, and `wake_settle_ticks` defaults to two ticks (including the detecting tick). Reconcile, health, and preflight continue during settling; `board_dispatch` reports `settling-after-wake` until claims resume. Both clocks are injectable for deterministic tests.
