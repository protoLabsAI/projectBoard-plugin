- **A nearly full disk pauses new work and tells you once.** When the computer was almost out of disk space, every card still tried to start, failed, was marked as permanently blocked and sent its own Needs-you page; freeing space brought none of them back. Now the board checks the free space before it starts a card and starts nothing on a disk with less than `min_free_disk_gb` (default 5, `0` turns the check off). Cards on other disks keep going. You get one page for the disk: "This computer is almost out of disk space (N GB free). K cards can't start until there is more room. Free up space and they will start again on their own.", followed by the waiting cards. When there is room again the cards start by themselves and the page goes away.
