- **The board view loads even while the board is busy.** Its listing reads (`/features`, `/cards`, `/cards/{state}`) are now served from a cache, keyed on the store's revision, instead of shelling `br` over the whole board history on every request and waiting behind the work loop's `br` calls. On protoEngineer, `/cards` had taken 5–19 s for 419 bytes, and the view's `/features` poll hit the hub's 20 s proxy timeout, so the board errored whenever it was working. An unchanged board is now answered at once. A changed board triggers one shared refresh, and if that refresh is slower than 2 s the view gets the last good listing (marked `X-Board-Stale: 1`) while the refresh finishes. `br` also runs with `BR_HISTORY_MIN_INTERVAL_SECS=3600` unless you set it, so it stops copying the whole `issues.jsonl` into `.br_history` on every write. That copy had grown to 1.3–2.8 GB within hours. Stopgaps for #686.
