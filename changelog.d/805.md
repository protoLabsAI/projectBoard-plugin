  Retirement uses the fresh batched done-card ledger to skip cards with no eligible
  locations. New reports and subsequently reaped trees are reconsidered every pass;
  eligible cards still receive a fresh read before retirement. Recovery protection,
  live-drive guards, failure retries and timeout propagation remain intact.

  and 10 open cards: consecutive retirement passes fell from 134 calls / 8.916 s
  and 134 calls / 8.683 s to zero calls / 0.000204 s and zero calls / 0.000193 s.
  GET /features cold/cached measured 0.412/0.00424 s before and 0.387/0.00421 s
  after; GET /features/<id> measured 0.0654 s before and 0.0634 s after.
  The listing already excludes archived detail hydration and its cached reads cost
  no br calls; API behavior is unchanged. These local timings do not reproduce the
  operator's loaded host or establish a bound on concurrent write latency.
