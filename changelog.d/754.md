Agents and operators can reserve a card’s worktree for manual resets, squashes, or rebases with `board_hold_worktree` and release it with `board_release_worktree_hold`. While the `worktree-hold` label is set, the loop skips claims, coder rounds, integration, salvage, recovery, base refresh, and worktree cleanup for that card without blocking it or paging the operator. Releasing the hold lets the next tick resume normal processing.
