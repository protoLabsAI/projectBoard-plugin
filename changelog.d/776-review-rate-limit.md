- **A rate-limited review waits instead of blocking for good.** When the review workflow failed on a provider rate limit (a 429, a spent usage or session limit), the gate retried every poll, spent its three attempts within minutes and left a `terminal` block that nobody lifted after the limit reset. It now blocks the card at once as `rate-limit` with the reset time recorded (the provider's, else 15 minutes), and the blocked sweep returns the card to review after that time. A review-gate block the sweep clears by itself goes back to review, not to a coder. A rate-limit wait spends none of the card's two auto-retries, so a limit that lasts several windows still ends with the card reviewed and nobody unblocking it by hand. The block reason now quotes the failed step's error, not only its name.
